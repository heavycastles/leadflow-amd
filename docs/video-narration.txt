[0:00–0:15 Hook]
Small shops in Uzbekistan sell through Telegram — in three languages. Every slow reply is a lost sale. This is LeadFlow AI, a multilingual commerce lead and order agent, designed to run on an AMD Instinct MI300X.

[0:15–0:40 Problem]
Orders, price questions, complaints and spam all land in one inbox — Russian, Uzbek and English mixed together. Owners drown in messages and lose hot buyers while answering price questions.

[0:40–1:30 Live demo]
Watch the live analyzer. We send an Uzbek message: Salom! 3 dona kurtka kerak, bugun olaman. Narxi qancha? LeadFlow detects intent order, language Uzbek, product three jackets, urgency high, drafts a reply in Uzbek, and creates an order draft automatically. Then we load demo leads: a Russian order jumps to the top with score one hundred, an English quote is next, a complaint is escalated, and spam drops to zero. Every score point has a human-readable reason — no black box.
